**Remote control from Apple Vision Pro**
- New in the sidebar: **Remote Control**. Switch it on, and the new visionOS app **AVP Play Remote** lets you browse your games, install and update them, and follow the jobs while you wear the Vision Pro. The Mac still does all the work.
- The Mac shows itself on your Wi-Fi. A headset pairs once with the six-digit code shown there; after that it connects by itself. Several Macs are fine, and an address can be typed in where the network blocks the search.
- Everything between the two stays in your network and is encrypted with a key that only your Vision Pro and your Mac know. Each headset has its own key; *Remove* takes one off again, *New Code* makes the code invalid at once.
- From the headset you see the same as on the Mac: the games with their filters, a game's options (favourite, your filters, version, toolchain, saved games, optional content, DLC sync, feedback), the jobs with their progress bars and log, Setup and Settings. Remote Control stays off until you switch it on.
- How to set it up: [Remote Control](https://github.com/myMartek/avp-play/wiki/Remote-Control) in the wiki.
